The top bar's count chip agrees with the verdict and leads somewhere. It counts the findings the verdict ranks (config findings included, evidence caveats left out), reads "No findings" only for a run the verdict calls clean, "Not fully checked" when the log lacked evidence and "Run failed" when a job failed, and clicking it opens that band of the Findings list. A "Skip to the verdict" link is the first Tab stop on the dashboard.
