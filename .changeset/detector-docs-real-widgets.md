Each detector docs page shows the dashboard's own widget for its example finding, with its charts and tables, instead of a plain card. A finding the run's verdict leads with appears as a verdict step ("What's happening", "What to try", potential savings); any other appears as a Findings row. Both open with the evidence panel expanded. The widget follows the docs page's light or dark theme and fits phone width.
