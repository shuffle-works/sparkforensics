The plan graph, the "Open plan graph" picker and Stage details hide info findings by default, like the Findings list. "Next problem" skips stages that only have info findings. The graph's Settings has a "Show info notes" checkbox with the count of info findings in the run. Stage details shows how many info notes it hides, and lists them when the dashboard's info impact pill is selected.
