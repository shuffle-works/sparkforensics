On a phone-width screen, opening a plan graph node shows its detail as a bottom sheet about 40 percent of the screen tall, with a grabber you can swipe down to dismiss, instead of a side panel that covered the whole graph. The graph pans to keep the selected node visible above the sheet, and the legend and minimap step aside while it is open. Escape and the close button still close it. At 640 px and wider the detail stays docked beside the graph.
