Every evidence card states its fix once. Slow Stage, Speculation Waste, Failed Tasks, Retry Waste, Memory Utilization, Partition Sizing, Autoscaling Churn, Job Failures, Config Audit, Excessive Small Files, Missed Broadcast Join and Oversized Broadcast Join print the fix at the top of the card, one line per kind of finding, the way the other evidence cards already do. Their rows keep what they measured: rows that had no measurement besides the detector's sentence show the measured part of it, and each Partition Sizing bullet pairs its partition figure with its estimate.
