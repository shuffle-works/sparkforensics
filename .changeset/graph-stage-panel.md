In the plan graph, a finding chip or a "Stage N" title opens that stage's findings beside the graph, in the panel the node detail uses (a right-hand dock, or a bottom sheet on a phone). The selected stage's box gets a ring. The panel shows the same tag, "What's happening", "What to try" and hidden-notes count as Stage details, and "Open full stage details" opens that dialog over the graph. Escape closes one level at a time: the dialog, the panel or node detail, then the graph. The tag in the panel links to the docs. Chips and titles have a larger click area, and the finding chips of a stage with no box of its own stay clear of the headers of other stages.
