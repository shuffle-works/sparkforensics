"Show evidence" opens the finding's evidence as a flat panel that is already expanded, so the evidence takes one click instead of two. The panel has a left rule in the finding's impact color and the card heading, no border, chevron or collapsed summary, starts at the finding text column in the Findings list, and stacks at phone width. Under a verdict step or a Findings row it leaves out what that line already shows: the tag, the fix sentence, the stage chip, the savings and the figures in the finding's sentence. A finding whose widget would show nothing more has no panel, and its button goes to the widget's card. The widgets are unchanged in the Full app report and the stage dialog.
