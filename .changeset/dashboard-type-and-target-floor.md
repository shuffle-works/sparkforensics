Raise the dashboard's text and control size floor. Text a reader needs is at least 12px (tag badges, the impact pill, table headers, chart category ticks, axis titles and legends, the verdict strip); only numeric chart tick labels stay at 10px. Short links and buttons, such as the chart Table and Copy actions, tag badge links, the critical-count button and the stage detail links, now have a hit area of at least 24px for mouse as well as touch, without growing their visible size.
