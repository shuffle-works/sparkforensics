Finding cards take their titles from the finding names the verdict and the detector pages use, such as "Slow Stage", "Small Files", "Cast On A Join Key", "Cache Utilization" and "Core Locality". A card and its evidence panel show the same rows, so a panel never opens empty and a card with nothing to show is not mounted. The stage table's Retried and Spill cells carry a chip only for stages the retry waste or spill detector flagged, in that finding's impact band. Fetch Wait and I/O Ratio show raw numbers.

The CLI and MCP evidence report treats a cache utilization finding that only says storage was not observed as a caveat, as the dashboard does: that check now appears under the checks that could not run, with the caveat's reason, instead of being left out of both check lists.
