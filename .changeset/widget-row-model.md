Finding cards take their titles from the finding names the verdict and the detector pages use, such as "Slow Stage", "Small Files", "Cast On A Join Key" and "Cache Utilization". A card and its evidence panel show the same rows, so a panel never opens empty and a card with nothing to show is not mounted. In the stage table, Retried and Spill cells carry a chip only for stages the retry waste or spill detector flagged, in that finding's impact band. Fetch Wait and I/O Ratio show raw numbers.
